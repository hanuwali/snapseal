      Questions

      How do I use it?

      Slide the power switch on, put the bag in the slot and press the sealing button. Use the separate opening button to cut a bag open. Instructions are included in the box.

      What's in the box?

      One SnapSeal Pro, a USB-C charging cable and an instruction sheet. The 4-pack also includes a USB-C wall adapter.

      How do I charge it?

      Use the included USB-C cable and any USB power source. Indicator lights show charging and power status. We don't publish a run-time figure.

      What bags will it seal?

      It heat-seals many plastic snack and food bags. Bags that don't melt with heat, like some paper or foil-lined bags, may not seal. Try a corner of the bag first.

      Is it safe to use?

      The sealing element gets hot, so keep it away from children and let it cool before storing it. The power switch helps prevent accidental start-up. Handle the cutter with care.

      Will it stick to my fridge?

      Yes, if the surface is magnetic. Some stainless steel fridges are not, so check with a fridge magnet first.
